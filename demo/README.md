# Demo Video Plan

Target length: 60 to 120 seconds. Use only staged, synthetic, blurred, or simulator content.

## 0 to 15 seconds: Why I built it

“I built Clipify after an earlier project called ChestCam. I wanted to explore whether an iPhone could surface possible moments worth keeping without pretending it understands what the moment means.”

## 15 to 35 seconds: Capture

Show the capture interface with a staged scene, such as a desk with generic objects. Do not show real faces, family spaces, schools, addresses, license plates, notifications, or personal photos.

## 35 to 60 seconds: Candidate moment

Show a privacy-safe signal or candidate-moment screen.

“Clipify combines uncertain signals into an experimental likelihood score. A result is only a candidate for review. The user can ignore it or keep it.”

## 60 to 85 seconds: History and reflection

Show key-moment history, episode grouping, or a diary screen with placeholder content.

“I wanted the app to help with what happens after capture too, so I started building ways to review moments and organize them into larger parts of a day.”

## 85 to 105 seconds: Privacy and control

Show capture controls, lock-state behavior, or settings.

“Camera and memory apps need clear controls. The app should show when capture is active and let the user decide what is kept.”

## 105 to 120 seconds: Where it is going

“Clipify is in TestFlight now. I am continuing to improve it with the goal of releasing it as a standalone App Store app. The automatic daily video story is still something I want to build, not a finished feature.”

## Recording notes

- Use staged or synthetic content.
- Do not show real faces, locations, private rooms, camera-roll memories, transcripts, diary entries, logs, or identifiers.
- Review every frame before uploading.
- Show what works today and be clear about what is still being built.
